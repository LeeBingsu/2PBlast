~~ Options ~~
This game has many different options, and I'll give you a quick explanation of what each option does:

[Display pressed Keys] - Shows in the top left corner of the screen the keys that you're pressing or not pressing.

[Allow BIG SHOT glitch] - When enabled, allows you to spam BIG SHOTS. When disabled, once you enrage Spamton, you will not be able to spam BIG SHOTS anymore.

[Allow spam] - When enabled, allows you to spam NORMAL SHOTS. When disabled, you have a limit on how many NORMAL SHOTS can be on screen at the same time.

[Allow ENRAGED mode] - If enabled, when you spam BIG SHOTS, Spamton will enter on his ENRAGED mode (where all the attacks will get harder). There's no point in disable this option if the "Allow BIG SHOT glitch" option is not enabled.

[Camera shake] - Shakes the camera if enabled.

[Show hitboxes] - Shows the SOUL hitbox.

[Distractions] - If enabled, will completely fade the BG and will slightly fade the HUD during Spamton's turn. Everything will be back to normal during your turn.

[Portuguese] - Translates the game to the best language, portuguese (PT-BR)

[Casual Mode] - This option does not work in checkpoints. When the battle starts, if enabled, gives you better items, reduce damage, increase the amount of shots that can be on the screen at the same time and reduce the time it takes to charge a BIG SHOT.

[No-Vibe Mode] - Prevents you from vibing, changes the song to a different one.
